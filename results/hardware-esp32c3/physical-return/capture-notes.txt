Capture notes
=============
All raw attempts are retained as boot-N.log with their capture controller output.

boot-1: Reached HW_DONE; all 196 HW_RUN lines were saved, but the raw log contains only 858 HW_LAYER lines. Its first line is a partial ESP-IDF boot message and its first saved layer record is layer 10. HW_DONE reports 868 layers and zero mismatches. summarize.py therefore marks the saved log incomplete/incorrect as a capture.

boot-2: No serial bytes or HW_BEGIN arrived during the capture attempt. The capture was stopped after several minutes of silence to recover the serial port; the zero-byte raw log and controller output are preserved. A separate esptool reset/check succeeded immediately afterward.

boot-3, boot-4, boot-5: Each reached HW_DONE; each has exactly 868 HW_LAYER and 196 HW_RUN records, with correct=true and mismatches_total=0. Their first raw line is a partial ESP-IDF image-segment boot message because capture started after esptool's reset command returned. All diagnostic records from HW_BEGIN through HW_DONE are present. The boot logs contain no saved HW_ERROR, panic, Guru Meditation, watchdog, or WDT lines; earlier boot bytes may have been lost before the serial reader attached.

No firmware was rebuilt or altered. The supplied image hash was checked before flashing, and esptool reported “Hash of data verified” after writing at 0x0.
