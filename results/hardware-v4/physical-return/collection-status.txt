Outcome and limitation
The v4 image checksum and flash verification passed, and the connected hardware was identified as ESP32-C3 with 4 MB flash. The collector repeatedly received valid V4_PROFILE records but timed out on profiles using model 1/backend 6. No capture completed the 96-profile training phase, so collect.py did not produce a frozen selection. Boot 2 and 3 under the required frozen-selection protocol could not be run. No firmware, measurement, or collector code was modified. All failed capture files and console/reset transcripts are retained. audit.py was run in hardware mode on the three v4 attempts; it is expected to fail them as incomplete.
