Outcome
The v4.1 image was checksum-verified and flashed unchanged once to offset 0x0 on the identified ESP32-C3 4 MB board. Boot 1 completed successfully with 96 training profiles, a frozen selection, 1880 tensor checks, and 400 runs. Boot 2 used the frozen boot-1 selection after an RTS hard reset but timed out waiting for V4_PROFILE at model 1/backend 6/sample 1. Metadata records last_device_progress (stage warmed; stack_min_free_bytes 4652). The bundle explicitly directs stopping after the first profiling timeout. Boot 3 was therefore not run. The hardware-mode audit accepted boot 1 and rejected boot 2 as incomplete. No firmware, collector, audit, measurement, or captured output was modified.
